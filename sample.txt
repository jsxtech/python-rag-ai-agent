Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence by machines. 
Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of AI that enables systems to learn from data.

Deep Learning is a specialized form of machine learning that uses neural networks 
with multiple layers. It has revolutionized fields like computer vision and 
natural language processing.

Key applications include:
- Image recognition and classification
- Natural language understanding
- Autonomous vehicles
- Recommendation systems
- Medical diagnosis

The future of AI involves more sophisticated models, better interpretability, 
and ethical considerations around bias and privacy.
